Upon first launch, you are greeted by our **Radial Command Console**. This is your central hub, styled like a futuristic watch face. Each segment of the radial represents a module.

- **Selecting a Module** — Hover over a segment and use the mouse wheel to cycle through its sub-options.
- **The Master Toggle** — The center of the radial is the Master Toggle. One click activates or deactivates the entire suite, acting as a **global circuit breaker**.
- **Saving Profiles** — You can save your favorite configurations as "Web-Profiles." Load them with a single keystroke via the in-game overlay (default: `F9`).
- **The Escape Hatch** — Press `F10` to instantly revert all active adjustments to default game values, ensuring a clean slate for online multiplayer or speedruns.
